Inside the book

Introduction

The Mormon Moment

Sometime in the last few years, you started noticing them.

Maybe it was a reality show about a group of glossy, feuding young wives in Utah whose religion turned out to be the least conventional thing about them. Maybe it was the young woman on your social feed baking sourdough in a linen dress on a picture-perfect farm. Maybe it was a coworker who politely declined the office coffee run every single morning, or a pair of impossibly wholesome young men in white shirts at your door.

Whatever it was, the question formed: Wait — what exactly is the deal with Mormons?

You are not alone, and you are not imagining the surge. This book is an attempt to close the gap between how much we see them and how little we understand them.

Fastest path to sale

Publish the eBook first (≈1–2 hrs of work)

  1. Set up KDP — account + tax & bank details (the real first-timer bottleneck).
  2. Update the TOC in the DOCX (open in Word, select all, press F9, save).
  3. Create the Kindle eBook — paste the title, subtitle, description, 7 keywords, and categories.
  4. Upload two files — the interior DOCX and the eBook cover JPG.
  5. Preview → price $6.99 → Publish. Live in ~24–72 h. Paperback follows the same day.

Only you can do these

  • Create the KDP account & enter tax/bank info
  • Update the Table-of-Contents field (needs Word/Docs)
  • Press “Publish” (I can’t access your KDP account)

Launch-week lever

Line up 10–20 ARC readers to post honest reviews in week one — enough early velocity to seize the “#1 New Release” banner in the small Mormonism category. Email templates (EN + ES) are in the package.
