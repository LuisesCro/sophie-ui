## 5. Description (HTML for KDP)

```html
<h2>You have questions about the Mormons. This book has 100 answers.</h2>
<p><b>Are Mormons Christian? Do they still practice polygamy? What's with the temples, the missions, the garments, and the no-coffee rule?</b> You've wondered — and now you can just look it up.</p>

<p><b>100 Questions About Mormonism</b> collects the questions people most often ask about the Latter-day Saints and answers each one in a page or two of clear, fair, jargon-free English. No preaching. No attacking. Just honest answers — with the myths busted along the way.</p>

<p>Ten themed sections let you read straight through or jump to whatever you're wondering about:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>The basics</b> — who they are, what to call them, and whether they're Christian</li>
<li><b>Beliefs & the Book of Mormon</b> — God, Jesus, the afterlife, scripture, and where it all came from</li>
<li><b>History</b> — Joseph Smith, the First Vision, polygamy, and the trek west</li>
<li><b>Worship & temples</b> — Sunday services, bishops, sealings, and the truth about "garments"</li>
<li><b>Rules & daily life</b> — coffee, caffeine, alcohol, tithing, tattoos, the Sabbath</li>

<p>Perfect if you're simply curious, have Latter-day Saint friends or family, are exploring the faith, or just want to finally understand what you keep seeing in the headlines and on your feed.</p>

<p><b>Open to any question. Get a straight answer. Understand the Latter-day Saints at last.</b></p>
```
