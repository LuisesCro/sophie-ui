# A Final Word

One hundred questions, one hundred answers — and if you've read even a handful of them, you now understand the Latter-day Saints better than almost everyone around you.

Here's the thing worth carrying with you as you close the book. The goal was never to make you a believer or a critic. It was to replace the caricature with a person. The next time you see the missionaries on their bikes, the neighbor who won't take the coffee, the influencer with the big family and the serene smile, or the lurid headline you now know how to read correctly — you'll see, where once there was a punchline or a suspicion, a real human being inside a real and fascinating story.

That's what understanding does. It turns strangers back into people. And that habit — asking honest questions, weighing more than one side, taking people seriously enough to get them right — is worth far more than any single fact about the Mormons.

Thank you for your curiosity. Go be a good neighbor.
