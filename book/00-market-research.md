# Market Research & Positioning — *The Latter-day Saints, Explained*

*Prepared for an Amazon KDP launch. Data: Helium 10 (US marketplace) keyword database + web landscape review, July 2026.*

---

## 1. The demand signal: a genuine "Mormon Moment"

Independent culture coverage in 2025–2026 (NPR's *It's Been a Minute*, TIME, Yahoo Entertainment) documents a surge of mainstream curiosity about Latter-day Saints, despite the group being ~2% of the U.S. population. Drivers:

- **Reality TV:** Hulu's *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives*.
- **TikTok "trad-wife" influencers** who are LDS (Nara Smith, Hannah Neeleman / "Ballerina Farm").
- **True crime:** the Ruby Franke case.
- **Celebrity memoir:** *Bad Mormon* (Heather Gay).
- **Consumer brands** with LDS roots (Crumbl, Swig, "dirty soda").

The net effect: a large, non-LDS audience actively curious about **who these people are, what they believe, and why they're suddenly everywhere.** That curiosity is the market.

Filtering the keyword database to Mormon-specific phrases surfaces clear, monetizable curiosity — and, crucially, **low title density (TD)**, meaning few existing books put these phrases in their titles:

**Read:** the *scripture itself* ("book of mormon") is high-volume but saturated and not a book we can or should publish. The **opportunity is in the question-shaped, explainer keywords** — "are mormons christian," "mormon vs christianity," "40 questions about mormonism," "how to leave the mormon church" — where demand exists and TD is 0–1. A well-optimized explainer can own several of these at once.

## 3. Competitive gap analysis

| Incumbent | What it owns | What it lacks |
|---|---|---|
| *Under the Banner of Heaven* (Krakauer) | Dark true-crime history; huge brand | Not an explainer; hostile framing; dated |
| *Mormonism for Dummies* (Riess/Bigelow) | Reference depth; respected | Reference tone, not a *read*; predates the current cultural moment |
| Devotional/LDS-publisher titles (Cedar Fort) | Insider audience | Not written for curious outsiders |
| Ex-Mormon / memoir titles | Personal, emotional | One person's story, not a map of the whole subject |

**The open lane:** a *current, engaging, respectful, myth-vs-reality explainer for the curious outsider* — the smart-friend-explains-it book for the Hulu/TikTok era. Evergreen in structure, timely in framing.

## 4. Positioning decision

- **Genre / BISAC:** Non-fiction, Religion — Christianity / Latter-day Saints & Mormonism; secondary Comparative Religion / Social Science.
- **Audience:** general, curious outsiders (plus book-club and "I have LDS neighbors/coworkers" readers).
- **Promise:** *In one clear, fair, genuinely interesting book, understand who Latter-day Saints are — beliefs, history, daily life, the hard questions — without an agenda.*
- **Voice:** warm, precise, journalistic; neither apologist nor attack.
- **Length:** ~40k words, 24 chapters + front/back matter (competitive with trade non-fiction).
- **Languages:** English first (larger market), Spanish twin edition to follow.

## 5. Why this wins on KDP specifically

1. Rides live demand (the Mormon Moment) with evergreen structure.
2. Targets 0–1 TD question keywords the incumbents ignore.
3. Fully KDP-compliant: original content, no trademarks, no real-person exploitation, clear independence disclaimer.
4. Broad enough for strong review velocity; fair tone protects against 1-star "biased" reviews that sink niche titles.
5. Natural series/expansion path (Spanish edition; companion "100 Questions" edition).
