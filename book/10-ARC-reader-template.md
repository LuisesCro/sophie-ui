# 📣 ARC Reader Templates (Advance Reader Copy)

ARC readers get a free copy before/at launch in exchange for an **honest** review in the first week. Early review velocity is the single biggest lever on launch-week ranking.

> ⚠️ **KDP rule:** you may give free copies and *ask* for honest reviews. You may **never** pay for reviews, trade reviews, require a positive rating, or review your own book. Reviewers should ideally not be immediate family (Amazon may filter those). Keep it honest and arm's-length.

---

## A) Recruitment message — English

**Subject:** Would you read my new book before it launches?

Hi [Name],

I've just finished a book I'm really proud of: **Mormons: The Latter-day Saints Explained** — a clear, fair, myth-busting guide to who the Latter-day Saints really are (beliefs, history, everyday life, and the hard questions), written for curious outsiders. Not preaching, not attacking — just the honest story.

I'm putting together a small launch team of readers who'll get a **free advance copy** now, in exchange for an **honest review** on Amazon during launch week (good, bad, or in-between — I only want real reactions). Reviews in those first days make an enormous difference for a new book.

Would you be up for it? If yes, just reply "in" and tell me whether you prefer **Kindle (.epub/.mobi)** or **PDF**, and I'll send it over. Launch is around **[date]**.

Thank you so much,
[Your name]

## Tips for building the ARC list (aim 10–20 people)
- Friends/coworkers who read nonfiction, book-club members, anyone who's mentioned curiosity about Mormons/Utah/reality shows.
- Relevant online communities and newsletters (ask permission before posting).
- Send the free copy as a **PDF or EPUB** (export the manuscript), *not* by asking them to buy — the review can still be posted; it may show as "unverified" but still counts.
- Keep a simple tracker: Name · Contacted · Said yes · Format sent · Reviewed ✅
